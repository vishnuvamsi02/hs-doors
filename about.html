Manufacturing Quality

How Every H & S Door is Built

We combine traditional joinery methods with modern precision tools to create doors that stand strong for generations.

1

Timber Seasoning

We hand-select premium solid wood (Teak, Walnut, Ash) and kiln-dry it until it reaches balanced moisture for maximum strength.

2

Precision Joinery

Our master joiners carve fluted patterns, panel reliefs, and interlocking mortise joints with sub-millimetre accuracy.

3

Hand Sanding

Every profile edge, corner, and rebate is sanded by hand across multiple grit grades until the wood feels like silk.

4

5-Coat PU Polish

We apply clear sealers, rich stains, and Italian polyurethane topcoats that protect against moisture, sunlight, and everyday wear.

Naturally rich in protective oils. Golden honey to deep amber tones. Immune to termites, highly stable in humid weather, and exceptionally durable.

Store Ready-Made vs. H & S Custom Doors

See why building directly with our manufacturing showroom provides superior durability, beauty, and lasting value for your home.

× Ready-Made Store Doors

Store Ready-Made Doors

Commercial mass-market doors sold off the shelf.

  • ✕ Fixed Standard Sizes: Requires cutting door edges or filling gaps with wood strips.
  • ✕ Hollow Core / Particle Board: Lightweight core that dents easily and swells in high humidity.
  • ✕ Thin Paper Lamination: Synthetic foils that peel away after seasonal rain exposure.
  • ✕ No Matching Window Frames: Doors and windows bought separately never share matching grain or color.
  • ✕ Multiple Dealer Markups: Additional retail margins added to your purchase.
✓ H & S Custom Doors Architectural Grade

H & S Custom Made Doors

Tailor-made for your home's exact dimensions and interior style.

  • ✓ Custom Millimetre Sizing: Fits your doorway opening precisely with smooth, airtight closure.
  • ✓ Solid Seasoned Hardwoods: Heavy 40mm–50mm thickness for high security, peace, and quiet.
  • ✓ 5-Coat PU Polish: Luxury Italian coating that resists moisture, sunlight, and everyday scratches.
  • ✓ Matching Architectural Windows: Harmonious window frames manufactured from the same timber batch.
  • ✓ Direct Manufacturer Pricing: Deal directly with the master craftsman on WhatsApp with zero middlemen.
