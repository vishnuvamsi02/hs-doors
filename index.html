Smart Buyer Guide

Why Custom Made is Better than Store Ready-Made

Many homeowners buy ready-made doors and face fitting problems. Here is why choosing H & S custom doors saves you money and trouble.

× Ready-Made Store Doors

The Problems with Store Doors

Often sold in hardware stores without custom fitting.

Fixed Standard Sizes: Leaves gaps on sides or requires carpenters to cut the edges, spoiling the design.
Hollow Core / Particle Board: Cheap hollow filling that gets damaged easily by moisture or accidental kicks.
Thin Paper Lamination: Peels off after one rainy season and loses its color quickly.
Middleman Markup: Sold through dealers with extra dealer margins added to your price.
✓ H & S Custom Doors Architectural Grade

The H & S Manufacturer Difference

Built specially for your home by master craftsmen.

Exact Millimeter Fit: Made to your exact wall opening. Fits tightly into your frame with smooth closing.
Solid Seasoned Wood Core: Heavy, durable, and strong. Provides security and sound insulation.
Multi-Layer Wood Polish: Water-repellent and termite-resistant polish that stays rich for years.
